About Identity after Work

Helping experienced professionals design life after work with purpose, structure and meaning

Identity after Work supports experienced professionals who are approaching retirement, recently retired, or beginning to wonder what comes next.

The focus is not financial advice. It is the human side of retirement: identity, confidence, structure, contribution and meaning.

Why I created Identity after Work

After decades of professional achievement, many people discover that retirement is not just a financial transition — it is an identity transition.

Questions begin to emerge:

  • Who am I without my role?
  • Where does my experience still matter?
