Identity after Work supports people approaching retirement, recently retired, or beginning to wonder what life after career is really meant to become.

Dave Hayes

Retirement coach for the human side of life after work

The focus here is not financial advice. It is the identity transition that can happen when the role, structure and professional status of work begin to fall away.

This work is for experienced professionals who want more than simply stopping. It is for people who want to design what comes next with purpose, structure and meaning.

Why I created Identity after Work

After decades of professional achievement, many people discover that retirement is not just a financial transition — it is an identity transition.

The calendar changes. The rhythm changes. The sense of being needed can quietly change too. Questions begin to emerge that may never have had space before.

Who am I without my role?
Where does my experience still matter?
What does a meaningful week now look like?
What comes next?

Identity after Work was created to help experienced professionals move from accidental retirement to a deliberate next chapter.

About Dave Hayes

With more than 25 years' experience in leadership, technology, coaching and AI, I understand the challenge of navigating change, uncertainty and reinvention.

I have a passion for developing individuals and helping people make sense of complex transitions. Identity after Work combines professional experience with a deep interest in the psychology of transition and the human side of retirement.

My focus is not financial advice. It is helping experienced professionals rediscover purpose, confidence and direction after career.
